# Repository instructions

For every user-facing website, product UI, or frontend component in this
repository:

1. Read and follow `skills/build-sarvam-web/SKILL.md` before implementation.
2. Use its recorded tokens and references instead of inventing approximate
   Sarvam colors, typography, spacing, or component treatments.
3. Preserve product usability and content truth; do not copy Sarvam customer
   claims, statistics, or unrelated marketing sections.
4. Do not commit Sarvam font binaries, logos, imagery, or customer marks unless
   Sarvam and every applicable rightsholder explicitly authorize that use and
   redistribution.
5. Run the builder theme validator and the normal project checks.
6. Read and follow `skills/review-sarvam-web/SKILL.md` before declaring the UI
   complete. Validate at 390 px and 1280 px at minimum.
7. Re-check the official Sarvam home and brand pages if work claims current or
   pixel-level parity; the bundled research snapshot is dated 2026-07-23.
