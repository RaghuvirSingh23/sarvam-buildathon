# Critical Sarvam web baseline

Snapshot: 2026-07-23. Use this compact baseline when the builder skill is not
available.

## Brand logic

- Vision: AI for all from India.
- Structural idea: a gateway between familiar and emerging worlds.
- Construction idea: mandala-like layering and repeated circles.
- Color behavior: continuous blue-to-orange transitions that indicate flow.
- Tone: grounded, familiar, modern, inclusive, technically serious.
- Avoid nostalgic spectacle and generic “Indian-themed” decoration.

## Geometry and rhythm

- 4 px spacing base.
- 1400 px widest shell and 1152 px common content cap.
- 96 px major mobile section gaps; 176 px at 768 px and wider.
- 44 px minimum targets; 48–52 px common CTA height.
- Full pill actions; 16 px mobile and 24 px desktop routine-card radii.
- 32 px large panels and 34 px desktop floating-nav radius.
- Sparse 1 px borders and subtle inset lines.
- Breakpoints at 640, 768, 1024, 1280, and 1536 px.
- Full desktop navigation collapses below 1024 px.

## Signature composition

- 32 px announcement strip.
- Fixed translucent floating navigation.
- Centered hero with motif, indigo eyebrow, display headline, supporting copy,
  primary/secondary CTA pair, and proof marquee.
- Large interactive product demonstration with pill tabs.
- Alternation between quiet editorial space, product detail, and evidence.
- White-to-soft-gray structured footer with semi-mono group labels.

## Motion and access

- 150–400 ms routine feedback.
- 600–1000 ms one-shot content entrances with small vertical movement.
- `.97` active scale for pills.
- Soft radial hover light on the primary CTA.
- Linear marquee only when a static reduced-motion version exists.
- Visible focus, semantic controls, 44 px touch targets, and no body overflow at
  390 px.
